# Echo demo visual identity

## Style prompt

A field notebook for audio engineers. Use a warm paper canvas, dark ink, and restrained green calibration marks. The layout should make the strongest evidence easy to read at a glance. Show actual commands, waveform intervals, and reported values. Put caveats beside the numbers they qualify.

## Colors

- Paper `#f5f4eb` for the main canvas and `#fffdf6` for evidence surfaces.
- Ink `#142822` for primary copy and `#4d5b54` for secondary copy.
- Pine `#126751` for an accepted result, the exposed ML condition, and waveform alignment.
- Amber `#8b4b09` for an abstention or case requiring review.
- Slate `#526d7a` for the comparison condition and reference signal.

## Typography and motion

Use system sans-serif for reading and SFMono or a system monospace for commands, filenames, and timings. Give text room to wrap on small screens. In the video, use short fades and vertical reveals. Hold each scene long enough to read without audio.

## Avoid

Avoid decorative badges, photographic backgrounds, simulated output, and charts whose axes conceal the scale of an effect.
